## Missing shots/assets needed to complete the UGC video
Currently, you lack the core components of a UGC video:
1. **The Hook:** A selfie-style video or dynamic shot of a creator introducing the problem or the product.
2. **Product Introduction:** Organic footage of the product being unboxed or held in hand by the creator.
3. **Usage/Demonstration:** Real-life video of the creator wearing/using the product in a natural setting (not a studio).
4. **Benefits/Proof:** Voiceover or speaking-to-camera footage explaining why the product is great, showing real results.
5. **Call to Action (CTA):** Creator telling the viewer to click the link or buy now.

## Recommended order of the best photos for creating the UGC
If you *must* create a video using only these static images (e.g., a slideshow ad rather than true UGC), here is the recommended sequence:
1. **Hook (Scene 01):** Crop the lifestyle portion (right panel) of the Smartwatch or Backpack collage to show a model out in the real world.
2. **Product Introduction (Scene 02):** Crop the product-only portion (left panel) of the accessories (e.g., gold necklace or silver bracelet) to clearly show what you are selling.
3. **Usage (Scene 03):** Crop the studio model shots (middle panel) to show the fit and style.
4. **Benefits/Proof (Scene 04):** Use the infographic elements (e.g., the features from the Linen shirt or STRYD sneakers) to highlight selling points.
5. **CTA (Scene 06):** Add a text overlay to the final lifestyle crop urging the viewer to shop now.
